SportLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

SportLog ("SportLog", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile app for logging the sports games you watch, predicting winners, and competing with friends. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using SportLog, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information needed to run the app. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising trackers.

  • Account information: your email address, used to create and secure your account and to send verification and password-reset emails. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form.
  • Profile information: your username, display name, optional bio, optional favorite team, and an optional profile photo you choose to upload.
  • Content you create: game logs, reviews, reactions, "watched via" details, spoiler settings, predictions ("picks"), and your watchlist.
  • Social connections: the accounts you follow and who follows you.
  • Technical information: our backend provider may automatically log limited technical data such as IP address and request logs for security and to operate the service. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, or device advertising identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To provide core features: logging games, making picks, scoring predictions, streaks, leaderboards, and following friends.
  • To display your public profile and pick record to other users, and your logbook to accounts you have approved to see it.
  • To send account-related emails (verification, password reset).
  • To maintain, protect, and improve the service and prevent abuse.

3. What Others Can See

  • Public: your username, display name, profile photo, bio, favorite team, and your overall prediction record are visible to other SportLog users.
  • Followers only: your game logbook (your logged games and reviews) is visible only to accounts you have accepted as followers.
  • You control what you post and can edit or delete your content at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

SportLog relies on a small number of service providers to function:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage (including profile photos). Your account and content are stored on Supabase infrastructure. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • ESPN — we retrieve publicly available game schedules and scores from ESPN to display matchups. We do not send any of your personal information to ESPN.
  • Apple — distributes the app and processes any transactions through the App Store under Apple's own privacy terms.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from within the app (Profile → account settings). You may also request deletion by emailing us at the address below. Deleting your account removes your profile, logs, picks, watchlist, and social connections from our active systems.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, secure on-device token storage, and database access controls that restrict each user's data to that user and the people they have chosen to share with. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Children's Privacy

SportLog is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. You can exercise most of these directly in the app, or by contacting us. We will respond to verified requests as required by applicable law (including the GDPR and CCPA where they apply).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of SportLog after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
